Brainlings

Study smarter with AI flashcards, bite-sized lessons and a friendly tutor, plus monsters to collect along the way.

Download for Mac

What it does

  • AI flashcards from a topic, your notes, a PDF, a PowerPoint or a YouTube video.
  • Spaced repetition that brings each card back just before you'd forget it.
  • Quick Revise and Test Revise, with flip cards or multiple choice.
  • AI lessons and an AI tutor you can ask anything, including about your own notes.
  • Voice answers: say your answers out loud, hands-free.
  • Google Classroom: see each class's announcements and upcoming work in one place.
  • Collectable monsters that boost your XP and coins.

Private by design

Everything runs on your own computer. The AI, your flashcards, your voice and your progress never leave it, and there's no account to make. Read the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Brainlings is a study app that runs entirely on your own computer. We (the people who make Brainlings) don't run any servers for it and don't collect, receive or store any of your information.

What stays on your computer

  • Your study data: decks, lessons, progress, coins, monsters, your profile name and picture, and your settings. These are saved in Brainlings' folder on your computer.
  • Files you upload (PDFs, PowerPoints and notes) are read on your computer to make flashcards or answer your questions. They aren't sent anywhere.
  • AI: flashcards, lessons and the tutor use an AI model running on your computer, either through Ollama or Brainlings' built-in engine.
  • Voice answers, if you turn them on: the microphone is only used during flashcard sessions, and speech is turned into text on your computer. Recordings aren't kept. A small diagnostic log notes timings and errors, but never what you said.

Services Brainlings connects to

Brainlings only contacts other services when you use a feature that needs them. Those services can see your internet address, as with any website, and their own privacy policies apply.

  • GitHub and Hugging Face, to download the AI engine and models you choose to install.
  • YouTube, to fetch a video's captions when you paste a YouTube link.
  • Google, if you sign in to Google Classroom (see below).

Google Classroom

If you choose Sign in with Google, Brainlings asks for read-only access to:

  • your Google Classroom classes (classroom.courses.readonly),
  • your classes' announcements (classroom.announcements.readonly),
  • your classwork and its due dates (classroom.coursework.me.readonly).

This information is only used to show your classes, announcements and upcoming work in Brainlings' Announcements tab. It's fetched directly from Google to your computer and kept in memory while the app is open. It isn't saved to disk, isn't sent to us or anyone else, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to train AI models. Brainlings can't change anything in Google Classroom.

To stay signed in, Brainlings saves a sign-in token from Google in a private file on your computer. Choose Disconnect in Brainlings' Settings to delete it and cancel Brainlings' access with Google. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Brainlings' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Brainlings is made for students. Because we don't collect any personal information, we don't collect information from children either.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Brainlings and deleting its folder removes everything it stored. On a Mac that folder is ~/Library/Application Support/Brainlings.
